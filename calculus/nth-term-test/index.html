Reminder: the nth term test

The nth Term Test for Divergence

If \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty} a_{n} \neq 0\), then the series \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}\) diverges.

If \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty} a_{n} = 0\), the test is inconclusive. The series may converge or diverge, so we need another test.
