Reminder: the Alternating Series Test

Alternating Series Test (Leibniz's Theorem)

The series \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}a_{n}\) converges if:

  1. \(a_{n} > 0\)
  2. \(a_{n}\) is eventually decreasing
  3. \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}a_{n} = 0\)

If condition 3 fails, the series diverges by the nth Term Test for Divergence.
